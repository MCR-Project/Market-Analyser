title: Paid In
summary: The opening amount plus every contribution — every dollar that ever went in.
---

## What it measures

Every dollar the account ever received: the opening lump sum plus the
running sum of every recurring contribution since. It is the account-side
counterpart to Final Value — one describes what came out, this describes
what went in — and their difference is Gain.

## How it is computed

<Formula tex={String.raw`\text{totalInvested} = \text{startValue} + \text{contributed}`} />

A withdrawal never reduces it: money taken out is reported on its own as
Withdrawn, so this only ever grows over the window. Netting the two would
let Paid In go negative for a portfolio drawn on for longer than it was
funded, and "every dollar that ever went in" would stop being true.

## How it reads

With no recurring contribution this is simply the opening amount, and
Total Return and Money-Weighted Return read the same — the account family
only starts to diverge from the portfolio family once this number grows
past the starting value.

## Caveats

Paid In sums the schedule, not the market: it says nothing about timing
luck (a contribution paid in right before a fall costs more than one paid
in after it) the way Money-Weighted Return does.
