title: Contributed
summary: The recurring contributions on their own, without the opening lump sum.

## What it measures

The same figure Paid In reports, minus the amount the account opened
with — useful when the question is specifically "how much have the
top-ups added up to", separate from the money the portfolio started with.

## How it is computed

One contribution lands on the first trading row of every new period
(monthly, quarterly or yearly) after the window's own first row — never
on the first row itself, which is the opening lump sum rather than a
contribution:

<Formula tex={String.raw`\text{contributed} = \sum \text{contribution.amount}`} />

<WorkedExample />

## How it reads

Zero with no schedule set, which is the default — a portfolio funded once
reports Contributed as 0 and Paid In as its opening amount, the same
relationship a single lump sum always has.

## Caveats

Says nothing about *when* each contribution arrived relative to the
market, only the total. Money-Weighted Return is the figure that accounts
for timing; this one is a plain sum.
