## What it measures

Only constituents weighing at least 1% of their fund are tracked
constituent-by-constituent by this app. This figure is the sum of those
tracked holdings' own weights — how much of the fund's total weight they
account for between them. The rest of the fund is real weight, spread
across smaller holdings, that this dashboard has no per-constituent
figures for at all.

## How it is computed

<Formula tex={String.raw`\text{coverage} = \sum_i w_i \quad \text{for every tracked holding } i`} />

The same total a fund's % of ETF column already sums to
(`measurements/official_measurements/etf_weight.py`'s own
`total_weight`) — this metric only gives that number its own tile and
doc page rather than leaving it inside a column's own computation.

## How it reads

Close to 100% means the fund's tracked holdings describe almost the
whole fund — every other fund-level figure here (Diversification Ratio,
Top 5 Variance Share) is describing something close to the real thing.
Well below 100% means a meaningful share of the fund sits in
constituents below the 1% threshold, and figures computed only from the
tracked ones are describing a narrower slice of the fund than their own
numbers might suggest.

## When the fund has been deep-filled

A Deep-fill fetches the prices of a fund's untracked holdings for a while
and lists them with the tracked ones. While it lasts this figure counts
every holding the window can price, so it rises towards 100%. A holding
the Deep-fill could not fetch, or any untracked holding when the window
reaches back further than the one year it fetches, is not counted as
covered; the response names each one, with why, instead of quietly
shrinking the figure.

## Caveats

Never null: a fund with no tracked holdings at all genuinely covers 0%
of itself, and that is the honest answer, not a missing one. This is a
coverage figure, not a completeness score for the *dashboard* — a fund
sitting at 60% coverage is not wrong or broken, it is simply a fund whose
weight is more spread out below the tracking threshold than most.
