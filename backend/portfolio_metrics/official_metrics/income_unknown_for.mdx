title: Income Unknown For
summary: Holdings the dividends table has no record of, named rather than silently folded into Dividend Income.
---

## What it measures

Which holdings, if any, Dividend Income could not speak for — every ETF
(since those live in `etfs` and never get a `ticker` row), and anything
resolved outside the tracked universe. Silence and "paid nothing" are not
the same claim, and this is the list that keeps the two apart: without it,
Dividend Income would quietly read an unknown as a zero, which would state
that SPY pays no dividend.

Never a toggleable tile — it surfaces as the warning-coloured clause at
the end of `PortfolioSummary`'s dividend note, only when the list is
non-empty.

## How it is computed

<Formula tex={String.raw`\text{incomeUnknownFor} = \{\, t \in \text{holdings} : t \notin \text{tracked\_tickers}(\text{holdings}) \,\}`} />

A ticker is "tracked" here if the `ticker` table has a row for it — which
every stock synced by `scripts/fetch_daily.py` does, and no ETF (or
anything resolved live for this one run) ever does.

## How it reads

An empty list is the common, good case: every holding's dividend history
is on record, and Dividend Income can be trusted as the whole picture. A
non-empty one does not mean those holdings paid nothing — it means the
figure above them cannot see whatever they paid, and the value and total
return still include it via the adjusted closes regardless.

## Caveats

A basket of ETF-of-ETF holdings, or one built entirely from symbols
outside the tracked universe, can report every holding here — in that
case Dividend Income is not wrong, exactly, but it is answering "how much
income can be attributed" rather than "how much income was there."
