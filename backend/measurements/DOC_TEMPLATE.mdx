## What it measures

One paragraph, in plain language, on what a reader is looking at when they
look at this column. Say what a high value and a low value each mean before
saying how the number is produced.

## Where the numbers come from

those getters quietly assume: a lookback window, a fallback, a rounding.
Those defaults are invisible everywhere else in the app.
