title: Revenue per Share
summary: How much of this holding's trailing revenue lands on each outstanding share.

## What it measures

Trailing twelve-month revenue divided by shares outstanding — a size-
independent reading of how much top-line sales each individual share
represents, useful for comparing companies of very different scale or for
reading alongside a per-share price.

## Where the numbers come from

`holdings` resolves the fund's holding list; `fundamentals` reads each
holding's `revenuePerShare` straight from yfinance's own `.info`
(`measurements/inputs/fundamentals.py`), live, with no database path.

## How it is computed

<Formula tex={String.raw`\text{Revenue per Share} = \frac{\text{Trailing 12M Revenue}}{\text{Shares Outstanding}}`} />

Read as-is from Yahoo, in US dollars per share, rounded to two decimal
places.

<WorkedExample />

## How it reads

On its own this says nothing about profitability — a company can have high
revenue per share and still lose money on every one of those dollars. It
is most useful next to the share price itself (a price/sales-per-share
reading) or against the same holding's own history.

## Caveats

<Warning>
Null, not zero, for a holding Yahoo reports nothing for (an ETF held
inside another ETF) — a zero would claim the holding generates no
revenue at all.
</Warning>
