title: Risk Contribution
summary: Each holding's own share of the fund's variance — a different question than its share of value.
---

## What it measures

Weight says how much of the fund's *money* sits in a holding. This says how
much of the fund's *risk* it drives — and the two can disagree sharply. A 4%
position that is twice as volatile as the fund and moves in lockstep with it
can contribute far more risk than an 8% position that mostly moves on its
own. A holding that tends to move opposite the rest of the fund can even
contribute *negative* risk: including it made the fund calmer, not riskier.

Every holding's contribution sums to 100% across the fund, so it reads like a
weight — but it is answering a fundamentally different question.

## Where the numbers come from

`holdings` for each ticker's weight, `price_frame` for closes over the
table's shared window. Only a holding priced for every date in that window
joins the calculation — see "Caveats" below.

## How it is computed

<Formula tex={String.raw`\text{contribution}_i = \frac{w_i \cdot \mathrm{Cov}(r_i, r_{\text{fund}})}{\mathrm{Var}(r_{\text{fund}})} \times 100`} />

The Euler decomposition of variance: each holding's weight times its
marginal contribution to the fund's variance, over the fund's own variance.
`services.stats.risk_contribution` computes this from the full covariance
matrix of the fund's tracked holdings directly, which is mathematically
identical to building the fund's own return series and reading each
holding's `Cov`/`Var` against it — the same helper the fund-level
diversification card (issue #105) already shares.

## How it reads

A contribution well above a holding's own weight means it is punching above
its size, risk-wise — concentrated, correlated exposure. A contribution near
zero or negative means the holding is doing genuine diversifying work,
however large its weight looks on its own.

## Caveats

Only holdings priced for every date in the window join the calculation — a
holding with any gap in its price history over the window (a recent listing,
mid-window) is excluded rather than narrowing the whole fund's window down
to fit it, the same reasoning `services/fund_metrics.py` states for the
fund-level card. Null with a reason, not zero, when fewer than two holdings
qualify or the qualifying basket has no measurable variance at all to
apportion.

<Warning>
This reads the fund's **tracked** holdings only (each at least 1% of the
fund). A fund whose weight sits mostly below that threshold is describing
less of itself than the contributions above might suggest.
</Warning>
