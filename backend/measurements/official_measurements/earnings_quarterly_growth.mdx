title: Earnings Quarterly Growth
summary: How this holding's most recent quarter's earnings compare to the same quarter a year earlier.
---

## What it measures

Year-over-year growth in a company's most recent reported quarter's
earnings — this quarter against the same quarter last year, which cancels
out ordinary seasonal swings (a retailer's Q4 against its own Q4, not
against its quieter Q2).

## Where the numbers come from

`holdings` resolves the fund's holding list; `fundamentals` reads each
holding's `earningsQuarterlyGrowth` straight from yfinance's own `.info`
(`measurements/inputs/fundamentals.py`), live, with no database path — a
figure that changes once a quarter, at each earnings release.

## How it is computed

<Formula tex={String.raw`\text{Growth} = \frac{\text{This Quarter's Earnings}}{\text{Same Quarter, Last Year}} - 1`} />

Read as-is from Yahoo and expressed as a percentage, rounded to two decimal
places.

## How it reads

A single quarter is one data point, not a trend — a company can post one
strong or weak quarter against an unusually weak or strong one a year
earlier. It says nothing about revenue growth, which can move in the
opposite direction from earnings in the same quarter.

## Caveats

<Warning>
A company moving from a loss to a profit (or the reverse) a year earlier
can produce a figure Yahoo cannot express as a meaningful ratio — shown
as a dash, not an inflated or negative number.
</Warning>
